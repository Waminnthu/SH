What are the standard delivery times? expand_more

Digital delivery is processed manually within 15–60 minutes after payment verification during business hours. You will receive credentials directly to your email address once our team confirms the transaction.

How can I track my order? expand_more

Once your order is processed, you will receive a confirmation and status update via email. You can also check the processing page for real-time status updates.

I haven't received my credentials yet. What do I do? expand_more

If it has been more than 2 hours since your payment was confirmed, please contact our support team via the chat button below or email us at support@shophere.com with your payment slip attached.

Can I change my email address after ordering? expand_more

Yes, but only before the credentials are dispatched. Please contact support immediately after placing your order if you need to update the delivery email.
